My Dashboard — Layouts, Gifts, and Wellness

1. Unzip the package and open the extracted folder.
2. Copy its files into the root of cpriss1979/my_dashboard.io.
   Replace matching files and merge matching folders.
3. Commit the changes to the branch served by GitHub Pages. Refresh the
   dashboard once Pages finishes publishing.

Home: Scroll below the cards for Edit Home cards and Arrange cards.
Drag cards into a column above or below other cards. You can also use the
column, up/down, and spacing controls. On a phone the columns stack.

Reflect > Blog: Select Arrange posts. Drag to reorder, or use Earlier
and Later. Set each post to one third, half, two thirds, or full width.

Shopping > Gift Planner: Save gift ideas with recipients, occasions,
dates, budgets, links, notes, and status.

Wellness > Exercise: Enter weight check-ins and a goal. Add exercises
from Workout library to My routine. Exercise photos load from the NHS.

Wellness > Calorie Log: Enter calories per serving and servings consumed.
Choose a date to review that day's foods and calorie total.

This ZIP includes the earlier sidebar, Blog, Notes, Reflect, and
Custom Palette changes.
