MY DAY — PERSONAL ACCOUNTS AND BUDGETS

Create an account with your name, email, and password, or sign in with Google.
Use Settings → Edit display name to change how the dashboard greets you.
Use the theme settings to choose a theme, custom colors, sparkles, and motion.
Signed-in theme choices and dashboard data are saved to your own account.

In Finances, choose Rename budget to give your planner a name. If you are
starting fresh, choose Start my budget and enter your first checking account and
its starting balance. Add income, bills, savings goals, and additional accounts
using the existing planner. Returning users can rename their budget in place.

Use the same HTTPS dashboard address and the same account to keep access to
existing data. Each new account starts with a separate dashboard. Export a
backup first if you want to move records from local-only mode to your account.

For the site owner
Extract the ZIP and upload its contents to the dashboard's publishing folder.
Keep the existing Firebase project. Enable Email/Password and Google sign-in
as needed, authorize the website domain, and verify that the deployed
Firestore rules restrict each /users/{userId} path to its authenticated owner.
The included firestore.rules is the reference for that restriction.
