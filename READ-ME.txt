GLOWCODE — BIGGER PLAY AREAS, WINDOW CLEANING & ACTIVITY SOUNDS

INSTALL
Extract and upload ALL package contents to your existing GitHub repository root, replacing matching files. Keep activities/ and assets/ in the exact relative locations. This is the complete dashboard. No build or Firebase setup/rule changes are required. It has not been uploaded to GitHub for you.

MOBILE
Game cards use more of the phone width, larger canvas play areas and safe-area bottom padding. Game controls & sound folds the shared toolbar away on phones, leaving core play tools accessible. Sand's 24-color palette folds into a color picker on phones. Desktop controls remain expanded.

QUIET CLEAN — REBUILT
An opaque fogged window hides a garden view. Choose 1 · Spray foam and drag, then choose 2 · Squeegee and drag over the foamed area. Wiping without foam does not clear the window. Tool width is adjustable. Fog window again resets it. Enable Sound under Game controls & sound for a short rubber squeak during an active cleaning stroke; it stops after movement stops. Keyboard: arrows move, Space or Shift + arrows uses the tool. Window foam/cleaning progress saves in the same activity state. Earlier patio/bench/fence/stone state is retained in the stored data.

RIPPLE POND
Pads/flowers continuously drift and gently bob within the pond when playing. Pause stops movement; reduced motion keeps pads static. Saved placement anchors are retained. Water has more layered reflections and surface highlights; flower petals have added shading. Sound toggles a flowing-water relaxation soundscape and starts off.

GLOW SAND
24 distinct named colors, retaining the original first five so old artwork keeps its colors. Draw/rake, undo and saved strokes remain. Sound enables a separate low, soft wind-like sand soundscape. The palette is expandable and closes after a phone color selection.

LITTLE WINS
Two optional deeper quests are added once, without replacing existing quests: Make something meaningful (a roughly 30-minute creative project) and One meaningful task (three manageable steps on a postponed useful task). No deadline, penalty or automatic completion. Existing medals/progress remain. Completing a quest with sound enabled plays an RPG-style ascending reward fanfare.

FIDGET SPINNER
Additional Starburst, Butterfly wings and Pinwheel shapes, six more colors, and Candy stripes/Confetti designs. Wind/whistle effects respond to spinning speed, becoming silent when the spinner stops. Reduced motion disables inertial spinning. All existing choices and progress remain.

OTHER GAMES AND SOUND
- Bingo: room ambience and spoken number calls when the device/browser supports speech synthesis. Visual calls always remain.
- Break glass: more architectural facade detail, shaded window framing and glass edges; city/traffic soundscape and glass-break effects. Existing midnight/fireworks completion remains.
- Blackjack: soft casino-style room/chime ambience.
- Bowling: a clattering pin effect only when actual pins were hit.
- Generic Test sound buttons are removed. Shared controls offer optional Relaxation on/off instead, alongside Sound and Volume.

All sounds are original procedural/synthesized soundscapes or device speech, not downloaded recordings. Sound starts off. Audio and activity loops stop when leaving the activity; hiding the page also closes audio. Enable again to resume listening.

NAVIGATION / COLORS
22 original, defined local SVG game icons and six pastel card palettes make the library easier to scan. Bookmarks have stable pastel colors, accent edges and title initials in both overview and bookmark views. Journal has eight more paper colors (12 total) and an Ocean sound on/off button. Journal sparkle implementation and keyframes are unchanged.
