GLOWCODE — ACHIEVEMENT MEDAL COLLECTION

INSTALL
Extract the ZIP and upload ALL contents into your existing GitHub repository root, replacing matching files. Keep activities/ and assets/ in their relative locations. This is the complete dashboard. No build, Firebase setup, or rule changes are required. This package has not been uploaded to GitHub for you.

DESIGN
Ten original SVG medals with coordinated scalloped metallic rims, ribbon tails, darker matching-color borders, gradients, highlights, decorative dotted edging and soft shadows. Every medal has its own palette and central illustration. Locked medals show the actual artwork with a gentle fade and an explicit Locked label. Earned medals show the bright artwork, an Earned label and a subtle static glow. Text remains dark and readable on pastel cards in both states.

The responsive collection shows the first five medals initially; Show all badges reveals all ten. The expanded choice stays open while choosing, editing or completing quests during that visit. Artwork is local and needs no external image service.

TRACKING AND PRESERVATION
Existing quest IDs, quests, stars, awards and completion history are retained. The legacy award ID creative still means Your Own Path; the new Creative Spark uses a separate ID. Previously earned medals remain earned.

Quest creation/editing now includes Quest type: Everyday little win, Creative, or Break / relaxation. New completions snapshot that type and whether the quest was created by the user. Saving, editing, choosing or skipping a quest does not earn a medal. The original unedited Make a little something quest is Creative; A moment away is Break / relaxation. Custom quests default to Everyday little win until explicitly categorized.

Historical completed records are used as evidence. The exact known original ID/title pairs for Make a little something and A moment away qualify for their corresponding new medals. Unknown or renamed historical quests are not classified by guessing from words in their title. You can set their type for future completions.

Lifetime achievement facts retain completed quest IDs and qualifying types even after the recent completion history reaches its existing 200-entry limit. Awards are permanent. These fields remain inside the existing Little Wins state, so they use the current backup and account-sync system. No separate service is added.

VERIFICATION
Automated tests cover every threshold and its boundary, distinct-quest counting, typed creative/rest completions, custom quest completion, no awards from saving/editing/keywords, legacy ID/history migration, permanent awards beyond the history limit, actual artwork in locked states, and show-all behavior. The ten SVG medals were rendered and visually inspected as a contact sheet. All prior game/account/sync code and assets were compared for identity. The whole dashboard index, including Journal sparkles and finance calculations, is unchanged except cache-version links.

DOM regression checks cover phone/desktop viewport settings, navigation, editing, saving, finance and mocked account sync. DOM tests do not measure real browser geometry. Live account sync and real-device rendering remain to be checked after upload. No real user data was read during testing.
