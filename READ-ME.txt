GLOWCODE DASHBOARD — PASTEL FINANCES
Updated November 7, 2026

INSTALL
Extract the ZIP. Upload ALL extracted contents to your existing GitHub repository,
replacing matching files. Keep index.html and its assets in the same directory.
This package does not contain your private browser records. Use the same website
address and account as before to retain access to those records.

BACKUP & DATA SAFETY
The previous complete source package is retained in the workspace at:
work/pastel-finance/before-redesign.zip
On first opening Finances, a pre-update dashboard-data snapshot is saved in local
storage, scoped to the active dashboard user. It is not overwritten on reload.
Backup & help > View pre-update safety copy offers Download, Copy and Select all.
The JSON works with the existing Restore backup control. Export a current backup
before restoring; restore replaces your dashboard data. If the safety copy cannot
be saved because storage is unavailable/full, changes are blocked with a message.
The normal Export dashboard backup and account/sync controls remain available.

LAYOUT
Money available retains its existing layout. Other finance records use stable
full-background lavender, blue, mint, peach and rose cards. Grids use 3 columns on
wide screens, 2 on medium screens, and 1 on phones; savings uses at most 2 columns.
Sections can collapse and retain their choices while the dashboard is open.

BILLS
Due Soon: overdue unpaid bills and unpaid bills due within the next 7 days.
Later This Month: other unpaid bills for the selected month (current by default).
Paid: paid/pending or cleared bills whose obligation falls in the selected month.
Upcoming: other unpaid dated bills generated through the next 90 days or selected
month end, whichever is later. Choose any month to see its generated bills.
Due date unknown: scheduled occurrences with no verified/estimated due date.
Bill rules remain available below the calendar, including unscheduled/archived
rules. Rules sort by next due date; individual bills sort by due date.
Click a calendar date to view and edit all matching bills. Filters also apply to
the calendar. Paid bills remain editable and pending until bank clearance.
Mark Paid records the sent date; Mark Cleared records the bank posting date.
Each refers to the SAME occurrence, so a payment is never generated twice.
An optional Estimated amount checkbox flags variable bills for attention within
7 days. Enter the actual amount and uncheck the box in Edit when it is known.
Recurring bills generate by stable rule/date IDs when viewed. History is retained;
unpaid occurrences carry over. No rollover job, destructive reset or duplicate
monthly copy is created. Saved occurrence edits do not change recurring rules.

MONEY & SAVINGS
Add Money and Subtract Money record actual account activity. Select the existing
scheduled bill/deposit when recording one to avoid creating a second transaction.
Move Money records one movement FROM one account TO another. Completed moves
update both sides but are never household income or expenses. It does not send
money through a bank. Existing planned/pending/completed moves stay editable.
Savings is excluded from everyday spending, bill coverage and safe-to-spend until
a completed move brings money into checking. Existing savings is not silently
split: set up Crystal’s Savings and Rick’s Savings using their actual balances.
Existing generic savings accounts and earlier savings entries remain intact.
Savings goals display the linked account’s balance against a target. They do not
reserve, divide, or move funds; multiple goals for one account show the same funds.
Who Pays What compares each checking account, its bills before payday, upcoming
income and projected available money. Expand a card to see its bill/deposit list.
Needs Attention shows overdue/near-due bills, estimates and pending transactions.

RECONCILIATION & CORRECTIONS
Accounts > Compare bank balance compares a dated CLEARED bank balance with the
recorded cleared balance on that date. It saves the discrepancy and a snapshot of
reviewed activity. Pending transactions are not part of a cleared balance.
No reconciliation result adjusts opening balances or transaction history.
Review discrepancies using History/Edit and compare bank statements yourself.
Finance changes save without confirmation. History > Corrections & undo retains
original/new values and can undo a change if its touched records have not changed
since. Conflicting later edits block undo; use Edit instead. Undo only corrects
dashboard records, not actual bank payments. Audit records stay saved and synced.

BALANCE RULES PRESERVED
Starting balance represents cleared activity through the end of its as-of date.
New same-day recorded payments/moves are tagged to that balance snapshot. Rebasing
an account does not replay those entries. Pending payments are projected once;
cleared items are booked once. Transfers never become household income/expenses.
No existing dates, amounts, account assignments or schedules are auto-corrected.
Income calendar rules, account cushions, and 30/60/90-day forecasts remain intact.
