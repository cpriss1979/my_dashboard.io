BREAK GLASS — HOW IT WORKS
1. A full oil-painted city appears behind picture glass inside a decorative golden photo frame. The glass has soft reflections and thin edges, without heavy window-grid bars.
2. Tap all 24 glass sections. Keyboard users can focus the play area and press Space/Enter to clear the next section.
3. When every section is cleared, an Open window into the garden button appears. The scene waits for you; it does not enter the garden automatically.
4. Press the button. The picture opens outward, revealing a flower garden, a path through flower-covered arches and a park bench.
5. The camera automatically approaches the bench, then tilts toward the sky. Bench armrests appear at the edge of the seated view. The sky gently darkens for the fireworks.
6. Fireworks continue without a time limit until New game is selected or you leave the game. New game resets the glass and scene. Leaving the activity stops animation and audio.

Reduced motion skips the camera journey and uses slower, gentler firework motion. It starts from the device preference and can be changed in the game. The previous Break Glass records are retained. City/traffic ambience stops when entering the garden.
