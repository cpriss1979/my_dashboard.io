Upload ALL files beside index.html to the GitHub repository root. In Firebase Console > Authentication > Sign-in method > Email/Password, enable Email/Password and Save. Leave email-link sign-in off unless separately needed. Google users should sign in with Google, then choose Account access to add a password to the SAME account. New users can Create an account. Forgot password sends a Firebase reset email. Forgotten email guidance cannot reveal account addresses; Google recovery is available through Google. Existing per-user sync rules are unchanged. No live sign-in or reset email was tested.
